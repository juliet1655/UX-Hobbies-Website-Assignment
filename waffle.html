Sample Waffle Stitch Square

Estimated Time: 30 – 40 Minutes

Materials

  • Medium weight (4) yarn
  • 5.0 mm crochet hook
  • Scissors
  • Yarn needle

Abbreviations

  • ch – Chain
  • dc – Double Crochet
  • FPdc – Front Post Double Crochet
  • st – Stitch
  • rep – Repeat
  • FO – Fasten Off

Pattern Instructions (Waffle Stitch)

Step 1 (Foundation Chain)

Make a slip knot and chain 24 stitches (or any multiple of 3, plus 2 turning chains).

This will form the foundation for your waffle stitch swatch.

Step 2 (Create the Base Row)

Double crochet into the third chain from the hook.

Continue working one double crochet (dc) into every chain across the row.

Step 3 (Begin the Waffle Texture)

Chain 2 and turn your work.

Work one double crochet into the first stitch.

Work one front post double crochet (FPdc) around the next stitch.

Alternate between dc and FPdc across the row.

Step 4 (Alternate the Pattern)

Chain 2 and turn.

This row alternates the stitches opposite to the previous row.

Work a dc where you made an FPdc before, and an FPdc where you made a dc before.

Step 5 (Continue the Pattern)

Repeat Steps 3 and 4.

As you continue, the raised squares of the waffle texture will begin to appear.

Step 6 (Build Your Swatch)

Keep alternating the two pattern rows.

Continue until your practice square reaches your desired size.

Step 7 (Finished Waffle Stitch)

Fasten off the yarn and weave in all loose ends.

Your waffle stitch swatch is now complete!

The waffle stitch creates a thick, soft, and textured fabric that is perfect for blankets, scarves, cushions, dishcloths, and other cozy crochet projects.
