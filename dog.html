Crochet Dachshund Dog Plush

Estimated Time: 5 - 6 Hours

Materials Needed

  • 5mm Crochet Hook
  • Brown Chenille Yarn
  • Cream Chenille Yarn
  • Black Yarn (Face Details)
  • 10mm Safety Eyes
  • Polyester Fiberfill Stuffing
  • Stitch Marker
  • Yarn Needle
  • Scissors

Abbreviations

  • MR = Magic Ring
  • ch = Chain
  • sc = Single Crochet
  • inc = Increase
  • dec = Decrease
  • sl st = Slip Stitch
  • FO = Fasten Off

Pattern Instructions (Dachshund Dog Plush)

Step 1 (Head)

Start with a magic ring (MR).

Crochet 6 single crochet (sc) into the ring.

Pull the yarn tail tightly to close the centre.

Step 2 (Head Rounds)

R2: inc ×6 (12)

R3: (sc, inc) ×6 (18)

R4: (2 sc, inc) ×6 (24)

R5–R9: sc around (24)

Insert the safety eyes before closing the head.

Step 3 (Body)

Begin with another magic ring.

Crochet the body using single crochet rounds until it reaches the desired length.

Stuff the body firmly before closing.

Step 4 (Legs)

Make four identical legs.

Each leg begins with a magic ring followed by several rounds of single crochet.

Lightly stuff each leg before fastening off.

Step 5 (Ears)

Chain the required number of stitches.

Work single crochet and half double crochet back along the chain to create long floppy ears.

Make two ears.

Step 6 (Tail)

Chain 8 stitches.

Single crochet back across the chain.

Fasten off leaving a long yarn tail.

Step 7 (Assembly)

Sew the head onto the body.

Attach all four legs evenly.

Sew on the ears and tail.

Embroider the nose and mouth using black yarn.

Step 8 (Finished Plush)

Weave in all loose yarn ends.

Your crochet dachshund plush is now complete!

Display it or gift it to someone special.
