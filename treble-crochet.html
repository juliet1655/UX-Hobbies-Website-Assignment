Sample Treble Crochet Square

Estimated Time: 20 – 30 Minutes

Materials Needed

  • 5mm Crochet Hook
  • Medium Weight Yarn (Any Colour)
  • Yarn Needle
  • Scissors
  • Stitch Marker (Optional)

Abbreviations

  • ch = Chain
  • tr = Triple Crochet (Treble Crochet)
  • sl st = Slip Stitch
  • FO = Fasten Off

Pattern Instructions (Treble Crochet)

Step 1 (Foundation Chain)

Make a slip knot and chain 20 stitches (or your preferred length).

Add 4 extra chains to act as the turning chain.

Step 2 (Begin the First Stitch)

Yarn over twice.

Insert your hook into the fifth chain from the hook.

Step 3 (Complete the Triple Crochet)

Yarn over and pull through the stitch.

You should now have four loops on your hook.

Yarn over and pull through the first two loops.

Yarn over again and pull through the next two loops.

Yarn over one final time and pull through the last two loops.

Step 4 (Finish the Row)

Work one triple crochet (tr) into every remaining chain across the row.

Keep your stitches even for a smooth and consistent fabric.

Step 5 (Turn Your Work)

Chain 4.

Turn your work to begin the next row.

Step 6 (Continue Crocheting)

Work one triple crochet into each stitch across every row.

Repeat until your swatch reaches the desired size.

Step 7 (Finished Triple Crochet Swatch)

Fasten off the yarn and weave in all loose ends.

Your triple crochet swatch is now complete!

Triple crochet stitches are the tallest basic crochet stitch and create an open, lightweight fabric that is ideal for shawls, blankets, scarves, and decorative lace patterns.
