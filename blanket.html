Crochet Blanket

Estimated Time: 5 - 6 Hours

Materials Needed

  • 5mm Crochet Hook
  • Medium Weight Yarn (Multiple Colours)
  • Yarn Needle
  • Scissors
  • Stitch Marker (Optional)
  • Measuring Tape

Abbreviations

  • MR = Magic Ring
  • ch = Chain
  • sc = Single Crochet
  • dc = Double Crochet
  • sl st = Slip Stitch
  • FO = Fasten Off

Pattern Instructions

Step 1 (Start the Square)

Begin with a magic ring (MR).

Crochet the first round using double crochet stitches to form the centre of the square.

Step 2 (Build the Square)

Continue crocheting additional rounds, increasing at each corner to maintain the square shape.

Fasten off once the square reaches the desired size.

Step 3 (Make More Squares)

Repeat the pattern using different yarn colours.

Create enough squares to match your desired blanket size.

Step 4 (Arrange the Squares)

Lay all the finished squares out in your preferred colour arrangement.

Swap the positions until you are happy with the design.

Step 5 (Join the Squares)

Sew or slip stitch the squares together one row at a time.

Make sure all seams are neat and secure.

Step 6 (Add a Border)

Crochet one or two rounds of single crochet around the entire blanket.

Add more border rounds if you would like a wider finish.

Step 7 (Finished Blanket)

Fasten off and weave in all loose yarn ends.

Your colourful patchwork crochet blanket is now complete!

Enjoy using it as a cosy throw, bed blanket, or thoughtful handmade gift.
