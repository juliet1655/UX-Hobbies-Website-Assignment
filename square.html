Sample Crochet Square

Estimated Time: 20 – 30 Minutes

Materials Needed

  • 4mm Crochet Hook
  • Medium Weight Yarn
  • Scissors
  • Yarn Needle

Abbreviations

  • ch = Chain
  • sc = Single Crochet
  • sl st = Slip Stitch

Pattern Instructions

Step 1

Make a slip knot and place it onto your crochet hook. Tighten it gently so it sits comfortably on the hook.

Step 2

Chain 16 stitches. Keep your chains loose and even to make the first row easier to crochet.

Step 3

Insert your hook into the second chain from the hook. Make one single crochet into each chain until you reach the end.

Step 4

Chain one and turn your work. Single crochet into every stitch across the row. Repeat until your square reaches your desired size.

Step 5

Fasten off the yarn by cutting it and pulling the tail through the final loop. Weave in all loose ends using a yarn needle.
