Crochet Flowers

Estimated Time: 1 - 2 Hours

Materials Needed

  • 4mm Crochet Hook
  • Pink Yarn (Flower)
  • Green Yarn (Stem & Leaves)
  • Polyester Fiberfill Stuffing
  • Floral Wire (Optional)
  • Floral Tape (Optional)
  • Yarn Needle
  • Scissors

Abbreviations

  • MR = Magic Ring
  • ch = Chain
  • sc = Single Crochet
  • hdc = Half Double Crochet
  • inc = Increase
  • dec = Decrease
  • sl st = Slip Stitch
  • FO = Fasten Off

Pattern Instructions (Tulip Bouquet)

Step 1 (Flower Centre)

Start with a magic ring (MR).

Crochet 6 single crochet (sc) into the ring.

Pull the yarn tail tightly to close the centre.

Step 2 (Flower Bud)

R2: Increase evenly around.

R3–R6: Single crochet around without increasing.

Stuff the flower lightly to help it keep its shape.

Step 3 (Create the Petals)

Work evenly around the top of the flower to form the tulip petals.

Shape each petal carefully to give the flower a natural appearance.

Step 4 (Crochet the Stem)

Using green yarn, chain your desired stem length.

Single crochet back along the chain.

Insert floral wire if you would like a stronger stem.

Step 5 (Leaves)

Chain 10 stitches.

Work single crochet and half double crochet back along the chain to form a leaf.

Make two leaves and sew them onto the stem.

Step 6 (Assembly)

Sew the flower securely onto the stem.

Attach the leaves evenly.

Repeat the process to make additional tulips for a bouquet.

Step 7 (Finished Bouquet)

Arrange the tulips together into a bouquet.

Wrap them with ribbon or bouquet paper for a beautiful finishing touch.

Your crochet tulip bouquet is now complete!
