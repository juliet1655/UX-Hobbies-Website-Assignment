Crochet Bracelet

Estimated Time: 2 - 3 Hours

Materials Needed

  • 2.5mm Crochet Hook
  • Blue Cotton Yarn (Flowers)
  • Cream/White Cotton Yarn (Bracelet Base)
  • Small Pearl Beads (Optional)
  • Yarn Needle
  • Scissors

Abbreviations

  • MR = Magic Ring
  • ch = Chain
  • sc = Single Crochet
  • sl st = Slip Stitch
  • dc = Double Crochet
  • FO = Fasten Off

Pattern Instructions

Step 1 (Make the Flower Centre)

Start with a magic ring (MR).

Crochet 6 single crochet (sc) into the ring.

Join with a slip stitch.

Step 2 (Create the Petals)

For each petal, crochet:

Chain 2, 2 double crochet (dc), chain 2, slip stitch into the same stitch.

Repeat five times to create six petals.

Step 3 (Make More Flowers)

Repeat Steps 1 and 2 until you have 8–10 flowers.

The number depends on your wrist size.

Step 4 (Crochet the Bracelet Base)

Using cream yarn, chain enough stitches to fit comfortably around your wrist.

Create two parallel chains to attach the flowers.

Step 5 (Attach the Flowers)

Sew each flower evenly onto the bracelet base.

Leave small spaces between each flower.

Step 6 (Add the Finishing Touches)

Sew a small pearl bead into the centre of each flower.

Add beads to the tie ends if desired.

Step 7 (Finished Bracelet)

Fasten off the yarn and weave in all loose ends.

Your crochet flower bracelet is now complete!

Tie it around your wrist and enjoy wearing your handmade accessory.
