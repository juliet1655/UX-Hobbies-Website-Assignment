Crochet Miffy

Estimated Time: 5 - 6 Hours

Materials Needed

  • 4mm Crochet Hook
  • White Yarn
  • Blue Yarn (Dress)
  • Black Yarn (Face Details)
  • Polyester Fiberfill Stuffing
  • 10mm Safety Eyes (Optional)
  • Stitch Marker
  • Yarn Needle
  • Scissors

Abbreviations

  • MR = Magic Ring
  • ch = Chain
  • sc = Single Crochet
  • inc = Increase
  • dec = Decrease
  • sl st = Slip Stitch
  • FO = Fasten Off

Pattern Instructions (Miffy Plush)

Step 1 (Head)

Start with a magic ring (MR).

Crochet 6 single crochet (sc) into the ring.

Pull the yarn tail tightly to close the centre.

Step 2 (Head Rounds)

Increase evenly around until the head reaches the desired size.

Insert the safety eyes or embroider the eyes before closing.

Stuff the head firmly with polyester fiberfill.

Step 3 (Body & Dress)

Start another magic ring using white yarn.

Crochet the upper body in white.

Switch to blue yarn halfway to create Miffy's dress.

Continue crocheting until the body is complete.

Step 4 (Ears)

Make two long ears using white yarn.

Each ear is crocheted in continuous rounds.

Lightly stuff the ears if desired.

Step 5 (Arms & Legs)

Crochet two arms and two legs separately.

Lightly stuff each piece before fastening off.

Leave a long yarn tail for sewing.

Step 6 (Assembly)

Sew the head onto the body.

Attach the ears evenly on top of the head.

Sew on the arms and legs.

Embroider the iconic "X" mouth and small eyes using black yarn.

Step 7 (Finished Plush)

Weave in all loose yarn ends securely.

Your crochet Miffy plush is now complete!

Display it on your shelf or gift it to someone who loves Miffy!
