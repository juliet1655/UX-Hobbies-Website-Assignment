Crochet Tote Bag

Estimated Time: 3 - 4 Hours

Materials Needed

  • 5mm Crochet Hook
  • Cotton Yarn (Main Colour)
  • Cotton Yarn (Optional Accent Colour)
  • Stitch Marker
  • Yarn Needle
  • Scissors
  • Measuring Tape (Optional)

Abbreviations

  • MR = Magic Ring
  • ch = Chain
  • sc = Single Crochet
  • hdc = Half Double Crochet
  • dc = Double Crochet
  • inc = Increase
  • sl st = Slip Stitch
  • FO = Fasten Off

Pattern Instructions (Crochet Tote Bag)

Step 1 (Foundation Chain)

3 ch (counts as dc)

2 dc ch 2 3 dc ch 2 3 dc ch 2 dc

ch 2

sl st to beginning

Turn your work and single crochet into the second chain from the hook.

Continue single crocheting across the row.

Step 2 (Create the Base)

Continue crocheting rows of single crochet until the base reaches your desired width.

Keep the stitch count consistent throughout each row.

Step 3 (Crochet the Body)

Work around the edges of the base to begin forming the sides of the bag.

Crochet continuous rounds using single crochet until the bag reaches your preferred height.

Step 4 (Create the Handles)

Mark the handle positions on both sides of the bag.

Chain the desired number of stitches for each handle.

Skip the same number of stitches on the bag before continuing the round.

Step 5 (Strengthen the Handles)

Work one or two additional rounds of single crochet around the handles.

This makes the handles stronger and more comfortable to hold.

Step 6 (Finish Off)

Fasten off the yarn.

Use a yarn needle to weave in all loose yarn ends securely.

Step 7 (Finished Tote Bag)

Your crochet tote bag is now complete!

It is perfect for carrying books, groceries, or everyday essentials.
