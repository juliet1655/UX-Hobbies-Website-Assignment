Crochet Scrunchie

Estimated Time: 1 - 2 Hours

Materials Needed

  • 5mm Crochet Hook
  • Medium Weight Yarn (Any Colour)
  • Hair Elastic
  • Yarn Needle
  • Scissors

Abbreviations

  • MR = Magic Ring
  • ch = Chain
  • sc = Single Crochet
  • dc = Double Crochet
  • sl st = Slip Stitch
  • FO = Fasten Off

Pattern Instructions (Crochet Scrunchie)

Step 1 (Prepare the Elastic)

Place your hair elastic in front of you.

You will crochet directly around the elastic band.

Step 2 (First Round)

Join your yarn to the elastic.

Crochet approximately 40–60 single crochet (sc) evenly around the elastic.

Join with a slip stitch.

Step 3 (Make the Ruffles)

Chain 3 to begin the next round.

Work 3 double crochet (dc) into every stitch around the scrunchie.

This creates the fluffy ruffled appearance.

Step 4 (Continue Around)

Continue placing 3 double crochet into each stitch until you return to the beginning.

The scrunchie should now look full and gathered.

Step 5 (Finish the Round)

Join the final stitch with a slip stitch.

Check that the ruffles are evenly spread around the elastic.

Step 6 (Finish Off)

Fasten off the yarn.

Use a yarn needle to weave in all loose ends securely.

Step 7 (Finished Scrunchie)

Your crochet scrunchie is now complete!

Use it to tie your hair or make several in different colours for a cute handmade accessory.
