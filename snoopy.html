Crochet Snoopy

Estimated Time: 5 - 6 Hours

Materials Needed

  • 4mm Crochet Hook
  • White Yarn
  • Black Yarn (Ears & Nose)
  • Red Yarn (Collar)
  • 10mm Safety Eyes
  • Polyester Fiberfill Stuffing
  • Stitch Marker
  • Yarn Needle
  • Scissors

Abbreviations

  • MR = Magic Ring
  • ch = Chain
  • sc = Single Crochet
  • inc = Increase
  • dec = Decrease
  • sl st = Slip Stitch
  • FO = Fasten Off

Pattern Instructions (Snoopy Plush)

Step 1 (Head)

Start with a magic ring (MR).

Crochet 6 single crochet (sc) into the ring.

Pull the yarn tail tightly to close the centre.

Step 2 (Head Rounds)

Increase evenly until the head reaches the desired size.

Insert the safety eyes before closing the head.

Stuff the head firmly with polyester fiberfill.

Step 3 (Body)

Start another magic ring for the body.

Crochet continuous rounds of single crochet.

Stuff the body before fastening off.

Step 4 (Arms & Legs)

Make two arms and two legs using single crochet rounds.

Lightly stuff each piece before sewing.

Step 5 (Ears & Tail)

Using black yarn, crochet two long floppy ears.

Crochet a small tail using white yarn.

Fasten off and leave long tails for sewing.

Step 6 (Assembly)

Sew the head onto the body.

Attach the arms, legs, ears and tail.

Embroider the nose with black yarn.

Add a small red collar around the neck.

Step 7 (Finished Plush)

Weave in all loose yarn ends securely.

Your crochet Snoopy plush is now complete!

Enjoy displaying it or gifting it to a fellow Snoopy fan!
